## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: I'm a foreign national and was arrested in Koregaon Park. What are my rights?**
A: You have the right to consult a lawyer immediately and the right to have your consulate notified. Do not sign any statement without a lawyer present. Contact us immediately — we coordinate with consulates and move for bail on an urgent basis.

**Q: What is NDPS Section 37 and why does it make bail difficult?**
A: Section 37 NDPS Act requires the court to be satisfied of two conditions before granting bail: (1) there are reasonable grounds to believe you're not guilty, and (2) you won't commit any offence on bail. This is a higher threshold than ordinary bail. An experienced NDPS lawyer can build the necessary argument — it is achievable but requires strong preparation.

**Q: Can a false 498A (dowry harassment) case be quashed?**
A: Yes, if the FIR is false, exaggerated, or motivated by ulterior purpose, the Bombay High Court can quash it. We've successfully quashed 498A FIRs where the complaint was filed as a tool in a matrimonial dispute rather than a genuine allegation of cruelty.
