## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: My company filed a cybercrime complaint against me. What do I do?**
A: Do not speak to police or cyber cell without a lawyer. Preserve all communications and do not delete any data. Contact us immediately for an assessment — anticipatory bail and FIR quashing are often viable in these situations.

**Q: Can a civil dispute (like a payment disagreement) become a criminal case?**
A: Yes — and it happens frequently. Cheque bounce (NI Act S.138), cheating (BNS S.316/IPC S.420), and criminal breach of trust are all criminal routes that frustrated creditors or business partners pursue. We defend all three.

**Q: I'm an NRI working in Hinjewadi on an OCI card. Does criminal law apply differently?**
A: Indian criminal law applies equally regardless of citizenship. However, passport impoundment and travel restrictions are a serious concern in criminal cases involving NRIs — we ensure bail conditions protect your ability to travel for work.
