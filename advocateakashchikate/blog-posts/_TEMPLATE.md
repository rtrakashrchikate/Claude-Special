## STRUCTURE

### Intro (150–200 words)
- Open with the reader's situation/fear, not with credentials
- Establish why this topic matters
- State exactly what the article covers (sets expectations, reduces bounce)
- One internal link to most relevant practice area page

### Body Sections (H2 for each major topic, H3 for sub-topics)
- Short paragraphs (3–4 sentences max)
- Use numbered lists for processes/steps
- Use bullet lists for options/considerations
- Bold key legal terms on first use
- Include at least one callout box with a practical tip or warning

### FAQ Section (H2: Frequently Asked Questions)
- 4–6 questions
- Write these as the exact questions people type into Google
- Answers: 50–150 words each, complete and self-contained
- Marked up with FAQPage schema (see schema/blog-article-schema.json)

### Lead Capture CTA (end of every post)
```
---
**Have a similar situation?**

Every case is different. Get a free, confidential assessment of your specific matter — Advocate Akash Chikate will give you an honest picture of your options.

[📞 Call Now]  [💬 WhatsApp]  [📅 Book Free Consultation]
---
```

### Related Posts (bottom)
- 3 internal links to related articles or practice area pages
- Anchor text must be descriptive (never "click here")
