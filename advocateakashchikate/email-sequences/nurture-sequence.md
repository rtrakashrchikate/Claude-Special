## MESSAGE 2 — Day 2

**Subject / WA opener:** 3 mistakes people make after receiving an FIR (and how to avoid them)

---

Hi [Name],

In 8 years of criminal defence, I've seen good people make the same costly mistakes in the first days after an FIR. Here are the three most common:

**Mistake 1: Talking to police without a lawyer**
You have the right to remain silent and the right to have a lawyer present. Every statement you make can and will be used against you. Even an innocent explanation can create inconsistencies that prosecutors exploit.

**Mistake 2: Trying to settle informally with the complainant**
Reaching out directly to the complainant — even with good intentions — is treated as an attempt to tamper with witnesses or an admission of sorts. Any settlement must be done through proper legal channels.

**Mistake 3: Waiting to see what happens**
"Let me see if they actually file a charge sheet" is the most expensive strategy. In the time you wait, the investigation gathers momentum, witnesses' memories solidify, and the window for anticipatory bail may close.
