## Family Law Services

### Divorce (Mutual Consent & Contested)

**Mutual Consent Divorce** — Both parties agree to separate. This is the fastest route: file a joint petition, attend two hearings (with a 6-month cooling-off period, waivable in some cases), and receive the divorce decree. Timeline: 6–18 months.

**Contested Divorce** — One party files; the other contests. Grounds include cruelty, desertion, adultery, and irretrievable breakdown. Timeline: 3–7 years (can be managed with interim orders and mediation).

**Applicable laws:**
- Hindu Marriage Act (Hindus, Buddhists, Sikhs, Jains)
- Special Marriage Act (civil/inter-religion marriages)
- Muslim Personal Law
- Christian Divorce Act
- Parsi Marriage and Divorce Act

### Child Custody & Guardianship
Courts decide custody based solely on the best interests of the child. We fight for the custody arrangement that protects your child and your parental rights.

- Sole custody / joint custody / shared parenting
- Interim custody at the first hearing
- Visitation rights (parenting time schedules)
- Child abduction prevention orders
- Guardianship under the Guardians and Wards Act

### Maintenance (Alimony)
Interim maintenance and permanent alimony under Hindu Marriage Act, Code of Criminal Procedure / BNSS, and the Maintenance of Parents and Senior Citizens Act.

We secure fair interim maintenance quickly while the main case is pending.

### Domestic Violence (PWDVA)
Under the Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act, 2005, victims can obtain:
- **Protection orders** — preventing further abuse
- **Residence orders** — right to remain in the shared home
- **Monetary relief** — compensation for losses
- **Custody orders** — immediate interim custody of children

These orders can be obtained at the **first hearing** on an urgent basis.

### 498A / BNS Section 85 — Dowry Harassment
**Complainants:** If you have been subjected to dowry demands and cruelty, we file the complaint and support you through the criminal process.

**Accused:** False 498A/BNS 85 cases are common. If you've been wrongly accused, we move for anticipatory bail immediately and assess the grounds for FIR quashing at the Bombay High Court.

### Maintenance for Children
Independent maintenance applications for children, even where parents are not divorced, under BNSS Section 144.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How long does a contested divorce take in Pune?**
A: Typically 3–7 years at the Family Court, with appeals potentially adding more time. However, interim orders (separation, maintenance, custody) are available within weeks of filing. Many cases settle at mediation, shortening the timeline significantly.

**Q: Can I get immediate maintenance from my husband?**
A: Yes — interim maintenance can be applied for at the first hearing and is typically granted within 4–8 weeks if income disparity is established. The court looks at the husband's income, lifestyle, and the wife's needs.

**Q: My husband took the children without permission. What can I do?**
A: File an application for interim custody immediately at the Family Court. The court can issue a production order and interim custody order. In urgent cases, a habeas corpus petition before the Bombay High Court is available.

**Q: Is mediation compulsory before divorce?**
A: The court refers contested matters to mediation as a first step, but it's not compulsory to settle at mediation. If mediation fails, the court proceeds with the contested hearing.

**Q: I'm a husband accused of 498A. What are my immediate steps?**
A: (1) Anticipatory bail immediately. (2) Gather all communications, financial records, and evidence of the relationship. (3) Assessment of FIR quashing grounds. (4) File for divorce if the marriage has broken down. We handle all four in parallel.
