## What to Do When You Receive a Cyber Cell Notice

1. **Do not attend** without a lawyer
2. **Do not delete** any messages, emails, or files — this can be treated as evidence tampering
3. **Do not give** any statements voluntarily
4. **Call us immediately** — we review the notice and advise within hours

The cyber cell notice is not a summons from a court. You have the right to engage a lawyer before responding.

## Bail in Cybercrime Cases

Most IT Act offences (hacking, cheating) are bailable or have standard bail provisions. However:
- Cases involving large fraud amounts may trigger non-bailable provisions
- Cases involving national security or critical infrastructure are exceptional
- "Digital arrest" FIRs where the accused is a mule often have bail available

We assess your specific FIR and move for bail immediately.

## FIR Quashing for Cybercrime Cases

Cybercrime FIRs are quashable where:
- The accused is falsely implicated (e.g. SIM/bank account used without knowledge)
- The dispute is civil (payment not received, contractual dispute re-cast as fraud)
- Parties have settled
- The FIR is vague and discloses no specific criminal act

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: I didn't commit any fraud but my bank account was used. Can I be prosecuted?**
A: Yes — money mule cases (where your account is used to route fraud proceeds) frequently result in FIRs against innocent account holders. The defence is that you had no knowledge of or participation in the fraud. We build this defence immediately, including applying for FIR quashing or, if arrested, bail.

**Q: What is the punishment for cybercrime under the IT Act?**
A: It varies: hacking (S.66) — up to 3 years; identity theft (S.66C) — up to 3 years; cheating by impersonation (S.66D) — up to 3 years; severe data theft from government systems — up to 10 years. Financial fraud under BNS provisions can carry higher sentences.

**Q: The police seized my laptop and phone. What are my rights?**
A: Police can seize digital devices under a search warrant. You have the right to a seizure memo listing exactly what was taken. We move for return of devices once the investigation is over, or challenge an illegal seizure.
