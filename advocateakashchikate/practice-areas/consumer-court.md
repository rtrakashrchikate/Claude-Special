## Consumer Cases We Handle

### Builder & Real Estate Fraud
Builders in Pune routinely delay possession, fail to register flats, charge extra amounts not in the agreement, and deliver substandard construction. Consumer court is often faster and more effective than civil court for these disputes.

**Claims available:**
- Compensation for delay in possession
- Refund of amount paid with interest
- Penalty for deficiency in service
- Damages for mental agony

### Defective Products
Appliances, electronics, vehicles, or other products that fail within the warranty period — manufacturer and seller are both liable.

### Insurance Claim Rejection
Insurance companies that wrongly repudiate claims (health, vehicle, life) can be challenged at consumer court. We have experience overturning unjust rejections.

### Banking & Financial Services
Wrongful charges, blocked accounts, fraudulent transactions, ATM disputes, and loan harassment.

### Medical Negligence
Hospitals and doctors who fail in their duty of care — consumer court provides a faster alternative to civil negligence suits.

### Educational Institutions
Refund of fee after withdrawal, false representation about courses/placements.

### E-commerce Disputes
Amazon, Flipkart, and other platforms refusing legitimate refunds or returns.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How much can I claim in consumer court in Pune?**
A: District CDRC handles claims up to ₹50 lakhs. State Commission handles ₹50 lakhs to ₹2 crores. National Commission handles above ₹2 crores.

**Q: What is the time limit to file a consumer complaint?**
A: 2 years from the date of the cause of action (the deficiency or unfair practice). Delay can be condoned with sufficient cause. File as soon as possible.

**Q: The builder gave possession 3 years late. Can I claim compensation?**
A: Yes — delay in possession is a well-established ground of consumer complaint. You can claim compensation at the rate specified in your agreement, or at a reasonable rate if the agreement is silent. Interest on the paid amount is also claimable.

**Q: Can I file a consumer case against a government authority?**
A: Yes, if the government entity provides a service for a fee (e.g. MSEDCL for electricity, PCMC/PMC for water, government hospitals). Sovereign/statutory functions are excluded.
