# Bail Application Checklist for Pune Courts

*By Advocate Akash R. Chikate | Criminal Defence Attorney | Bombay High Court*

---

## Before You Approach a Lawyer

- [ ] Get a copy of the FIR (police station or online portal)
- [ ] Note the police station name and case/crime number
- [ ] Note exactly when the arrest happened (date and time)
- [ ] Note which court the accused was produced before
- [ ] Know the sections of law the accused is charged under

---

## Documents to Collect for Bail Application

### Accused's Personal Documents
- [ ] Aadhaar card (address proof)
- [ ] Voter ID or Passport (identity proof)
- [ ] PAN card
- [ ] 2–4 passport size photographs

### Proof of Roots in Pune (Critical — courts want to see these)
- [ ] Property documents (ownership or rent agreement) showing Pune residence
- [ ] Employment letter or business registration (shows ties to the city)
- [ ] Bank passbook (6 months) showing regular transactions from Pune
- [ ] Utility bills (electricity, water) in accused's name
- [ ] Children's school admission form (showing family is settled in Pune)

### Surety Documents (for the person providing bail guarantee)
- [ ] Aadhaar card of surety person
- [ ] Property documents showing surety's property in Pune
- [ ] Recent bank statement of surety (showing financial stability)
- [ ] Photographs (2–4) of surety

### Case-Specific Documents
- [ ] Any documents contradicting the FIR allegations
- [ ] Medical reports if accused has a health condition
- [ ] Prior clean record certificate (if accused has no criminal history — lawyer can get this)
- [ ] Character certificates from respectable persons if available

---

## What the Lawyer Will Do

1. Review the FIR and assess the correct court (Magistrate / Sessions / HC)
2. Draft the bail application with all required facts and legal submissions
3. File the application the same day (or next morning if court is closed)
4. Appear before the court and argue for bail
5. Handle surety formalities once bail is granted

---

## After Bail is Granted — Your Obligations

- [ ] Note ALL conditions imposed (conditions are binding — violation means cancellation)
- [ ] Surrender passport if ordered
- [ ] Report to the specified police station on the scheduled days
- [ ] Do NOT leave the city/state without court permission
- [ ] Appear in court on all listed dates — missing a date = bail cancellation risk
- [ ] Do NOT contact the complainant or witnesses

---

## What to Do If Bail is Refused

- [ ] Do not lose hope — one refusal is not the end
- [ ] File immediately at the next higher court (Sessions → High Court)
- [ ] The lawyer will file a detailed application addressing the specific reasons given for refusal
